Davhana AutoCare Privacy Notice

Version 1.0 · 16 November 2026

Before live bookings: Confirm the legal entity, privacy contact, retention period, device/server storage design and any service providers. This booking page currently saves demo requests in the browser's local storage on the same device; no booking notification is sent to the mechanic.

1. Responsible party

Davhana AutoCare is the intended responsible party for booking information. Its legal entity name and verified Information Officer/privacy contact must be published before live collection.

2. Information collected

The booking form may collect your name, telephone number, vehicle details, preferred date and time, description of the vehicle issue and other details you choose to provide.

3. Purposes and lawful basis

Information is used to receive, assess, manage and respond to a booking request, communicate about the requested vehicle service and meet applicable legal obligations. The business must document the lawful basis for each purpose. Acknowledging this notice confirms you have been informed; it is not consent to unrelated marketing.

4. Storage and security

The current demonstration stores booking form entries in local storage in your browser on this device. Anyone with access to this browser profile or device may be able to access those entries. Avoid using a shared device and clear browser data when appropriate. Before a live server-connected booking service is launched, implement server-side access controls, encryption, backups, retention rules and operator agreements.

5. Retention and sharing

Booking details should be accessible only to authorised personnel and retained only as long as necessary for the booking and legal obligations. Do not share vehicle or contact information with unrelated parties. Any service provider used by a future live booking system must be disclosed and contractually controlled.

6. Your rights

Subject to POPIA, you may request access to or correction of your information, object to certain processing and request deletion where legally permitted. Add the business's verified privacy contact here before accepting live bookings. You may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.
